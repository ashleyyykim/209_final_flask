Teams whose role players increase their PIE from the regular season to the playoffs tend to win more playoff games

This section examines whether teams that got stronger role-player contributions in the playoffs tended to advance further. The combined view brings together the scatterplot and boxplot so the reader can see both the team-level relationship and the broader distribution of role-player impact in one place.

03

Do Stars Rise to the Moment in Playoff Wins?

The intuition is simple: when a team wins a playoff series, its star must have risen to the occasion. This would mean outplaying his regular-season self while the losing team's star faltered. This visualization sets out to explore whether the defining star on each team (its highest regular-season PIE player) really does perform better, relative to his own regular season, in series his team wins versus loses during the playoffs.
